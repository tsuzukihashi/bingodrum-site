2. 端末の中で扱うもの

大会大会の名前、玉の数と範囲、型、テーマ、出た番号と順番と時刻、リーチの人数、カードを作るための種
ビンゴと景品景品の名前と、入れたときだけ写真、当たったカードの番号と何玉目か、入れたときだけ当たった人の名前
アプリの設定読み上げの言葉と速さ、効果音、BINGO の字の有り無し、回る長さ、既定の玉の数、テーマ、操作のロックのオン・オフ、案内を終えたかどうか、レビューのお願いを出した版

3. 通信と、入れていないもの

本アプリは通信をしません（Android 版はインターネットの権限も持ちません）。テレビやプロジェクターへの表示は、AirPlay・HDMI・キャストなど端末の機能で行います。広告・アプリ内課金・アカウント登録・分析ツール・クラッシュの報告は入れていません。

4. 読み上げと音

読み上げは端末の中の音声で行い、文や番号を外へ送りません。効果音はアプリの中で作った音です。

5. 購入

本アプリは App Store と Google Play の有料アプリです。お支払いは Apple・Google が処理し、支払いの情報は開発者には届きません。

6. 情報を使う目的

上のものは、アプリを動かすためだけに使います。お問い合わせをいただいたときは、メールアドレスと内容をお返事のためだけに使います。法令にもとづく場合を除き、本人の同意なく第三者に渡すことはありません。

7. 消したいとき

大会と結果は、アプリの大会の一覧から1つずつ、または設定の「記録を全部消す」で消せます。アプリを消すと、端末の中のものはすべて消えます。

8. 幹事の方へ

当たった人の名前を入れるときは、必要なときだけ入れてください。テレビの画面に名前が出ることがあります。

9. このウェブサイトについて

10. 改定

Privacy Policy

BingoDrum · Effective November 17, 2026 · Last updated November 17, 2026

Summary
BingoDrum has no developer server and doesn't connect to the internet. There is no sign-up or sign-in, and the developer never receives information about you or your players. There are no ads, analytics or crash reporting (the only outside component is Google Play's in-app review in the Android version). Games, prizes and results stay on your device.

1. Provider

ProviderRyo Tsuzukihashi (independent developer / TsuzuKit)
AppBingoDrum (Japanese name: ガラガラビンゴ)
ScopeThe iPhone, iPad and Android app, and pages under https://bingodrum.tsuzukit.com/, including this one
Contactryo.tsuzukihashi@tsuzukit.com

2. What stays on your device

GamesGame name, number of balls and range, pattern, theme, called numbers with order and time, reach count, and the seed used to make cards
Bingos and prizesPrize names and, only if you add them, photos; winning card numbers and the ball they won on; and, only if you enter them, winners' names
SettingsVoice wording and speed, sound effects, B-I-N-G-O letters on or off, spin length, default number of balls, theme, whether Control lock is on, whether you finished the introduction, and the version in which we last asked for a review

All of this is stored only on your device. Nothing is sent to a developer server or to iCloud (it may be included in your device backup, which the developer cannot see). Prize photos are only the ones you pick from your photo library, copied into the app.

3. Network use and what is not included

The app doesn't connect to the internet (the Android version doesn't even have the internet permission). Showing it on a TV or projector uses your device's own features such as AirPlay, HDMI or casting. There are no ads, in-app purchases, accounts, analytics or crash reporting.

4. Voice and sound

Numbers are called with the voices on your device; no text or numbers are sent anywhere. Sound effects are generated inside the app.

5. Purchase

BingoDrum is a paid app on the App Store and Google Play. Payments are handled by Apple and Google; payment details never reach the developer.

6. How information is used

The above is used only to run the app. If you contact us, we use your email address and message only to reply. We never share information with third parties without consent, except as required by law.

7. Deleting your data

Delete games one by one from the game list, or all at once in Settings › Erase all records. Deleting the app removes everything on the device.

8. For hosts

Enter winners' names only when needed. Names may appear on the TV screen.

9. This website

This site (bingodrum.tsuzukit.com) uses no cookies, no analytics and no ads. It is served by GitHub Pages, and delivery logs are handled under GitHub's General Privacy Statement.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.
